🎯 Why This Manual

The two longest threads on TripAdvisor's Jeju forum are both some version of "please recommend a taxi driver" (74 and 60 replies). Seniors, families, and solo travelers who don't or can't drive keep asking "buses are slow and renting a car is scary — how do I get around?", and the answers pile up as personal drivers' phone numbers. But Jeju actually runs two official tourist-taxi programs, with published rate cards — use those instead of a stranger's number. For getting around by bus, see #1212; this manual covers taxis and private charters.

★ 2 Official Tourist-Taxi Programs — Rate Cards

1) Jeju Global Taxi (제주 글로벌 택시) — English/Chinese/Japanese dedicated, the province's only officially certified channel for foreign visitors

Vehicle Capacity 8 Hours Overtime
Standard 4 ₩220,000 +₩30,000/hr
Jumbo 5–7 ₩330,000 +₩40,000/hr
Luxury Jumbo 5–6 ₩400,000 +₩50,000/hr
Big Van 7–12 ₩450,000 +₩60,000/hr
  • Fuel, insurance, parking, and the driver's lunch are all included — no tipping. Beyond 30km, +₩30,000. Booking line: 1899-4314 (English/Chinese/Japanese).

2) Jeju Tourism Happy Taxi (제주관광행복택시) — province-run central booking (064-120), mostly Korean-speaking, priced by the hour so it works well for half-day trips

Category Up to 3 hrs 3–5 hrs 5–9 hrs Overtime
Midsize (4) ₩60,000 ₩90,000 ₩170,000 ₩10,000/30min
Large (9) ₩90,000 ₩140,000 ₩250,000 ₩15,000/30min
  • Parking and admission fees are separate. The program's own guidelines state "no additional cash requested."

Which one? — If you need an English-, Chinese-, or Japanese-speaking driver, go with Jeju Global Taxi; if you have a Korean speaker along and only need a half day, go with Jeju Tourism Happy Taxi. There's no official explanation for why the two rates differ this much (likely driver language and what's included), so when booking, ask once what's included.

  • Airport → Seogwipo (서귀포): about 40–50 minutes, ₩35,000–55,000 (varies by destination and vehicle). At the arrivals-level exit, regular-taxi and large-taxi stands are separate. A 9-seat jumbo needs a separate phone-booking company, so reserve ahead if your group is large.
  • There's no official source on 4–5am taxi availability — for that window, book the day before.

🗺 3 Standard Day Routes + Watch Out for Udo

Per Jeju Global Taxi's official page, the standard options are East, West, and South routes, plus custom.

  • East: Seongsan Ilchulbong (성산일출봉) → Seopjikoji (섭지코지) → Haenyeo Museum (해녀박물관) (the official sample combination). Add sunrise and you're leaving early.
  • West: A Hyeopjae Beach (협재 해변), Osulloc (오설록), and Hallim (한림) combination is common (not confirmed as an official sample).
  • South: A Jungmun (중문), Cheonjiyeon (천지연), and downtown Seogwipo combination is common.
  • Hallasan (한라산): There's no set convention for hiking pickups — agree on round-trip timing with your driver in advance.
  • ⚠ Udo (우도) is off-limits to taxis and regular rental cars (banned since August 2017; through July 2026 there's a temporary exception for Category-1 eco-friendly rental cars and charter buses of 16 seats or fewer). Take the ferry from Seongsan Port (성산항) and get around the island by circular bus or electric-vehicle rental. Either have your taxi wait at the port, or dedicate that day to Udo alone.

⚠ 4 Traps

1. Personal driver numbers from forums — indistinguishable from unlicensed operators ("call-hailing"). Yellow license plates = licensed commercial taxis (under the Passenger Transport Service Act), and both official programs and the platforms above are all licensed. A white-plate car's "tour" has no insurance.

2. Two different rate cards — the same 8 hours costing different amounts isn't a rip-off; they're different programs. On the booking call, ask what's included (fuel, parking, meals) and get the total in a text message.

3. Requests for extra cash — banned under Jeju Tourism Happy Taxi's rules, and Jeju Global Taxi's price is already all-inclusive. If asked, confirm on the spot with the booking line (064-120 / 1899-4314).

4. Udo and Marado (마라도) — put an island taxis can't reach into your day route and you're just paying the driver to wait (see above).

🚗 Renting a Car Instead — 3 Conditions That Trip Up Foreigners (#249 · #439)

1. International driving permit: Korea only recognizes IDPs issued under the 1949 Geneva Convention (Korea hasn't ratified the 1968 Vienna Convention). Privately sold "International Driving License" cards are invalid. Bring it together with your home license and passport.

2. A credit card in your own name is required: a security hold beyond the rental fee gets placed on it. Many companies won't accept debit cards or cash — "can I do this without a credit card?" is usually no.

3. Collision damage waiver: there are 4 tiers — standard, full, partial-unlimited, and super-unlimited. Some companies only offer foreign renters the unlimited tier, which raises the quote. EVs also require a charging plan.

If driving feels like a burden, the taxi tours above sidestep all three.

⚙ 3 FAQs

1. What does a full-day taxi tour cost for two people? — Jeju Global Taxi's standard 4-seater, 8 hours, ₩220,000 (all-inclusive), or Jeju Tourism Happy Taxi's midsize, 5–9 hours, ₩170,000 (+ parking and admission). Roughly ₩100,000 per person.

2. Where do I find an English-speaking driver? — Jeju Global Taxi (1899-4314), or the driver-language options on KKday/Klook. Don't expect it from regular taxis or Kakao T.

3. Can a taxi tour start right from the airport? — Book ahead and it starts with airport pickup. Your luggage goes in the trunk, and you can route through your accommodation as part of the course.
