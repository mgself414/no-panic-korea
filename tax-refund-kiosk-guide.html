🎯 How to Read This Procedure Sheet — Screen Notation Rules

This piece isn't "what tax refund is" — it writes down exactly "what to tap on the kiosk screen, in what order." The instant-refund vs. later-refund distinction, minimum purchase amount, refund rate, and downtown-refund strategy were already covered in #1231 — this piece doesn't repeat them.

  • Buttons on screen appear in brackets: Refund (환급)
  • Screen titles and notices appear in corner brackets: 「segwan hwagin daesang (세관 확인 대상) — Customs check required」
  • Actions you do by hand are bold: "place your passport flat on the scanner"

① Why "Eligibility" and "Procedure" Need to Be Read Separately

#1231 covered the full picture of eligibility — the instant-refund vs. later-refund distinction, the minimum amount (₩15,000+), the refund rate (roughly 7–8%), and downtown-refund strategy. This piece assumes you already know all that, and covers only what actually happens in front of the screen: the instant-refund moment at the store counter, the exact scan order at the airport kiosk, the 「Customs check required」 branch, and terminal-specific pickup routes. If you're still fuzzy on eligibility and strategy overall, read #1231 Foreign Visitor Tax Refund Guide first — that's the right order.

② The Store Instant-Refund Moment — What Actually Happens at the Counter

  • Before you pay, pull out your passport and show it first — hand it over before staff even ask, and the whole thing wraps up in one pass.
  • Staff use your passport info to calculate the VAT-deducted price → you pay that already-deducted amount, as-is. No separate refund application needed.
  • Limits: ≤₩1,000,000 per single payment, ≤₩5,000,000 cumulative for the whole trip.
  • Go over the limit and it doesn't mean "no refund" — it just means the processing method automatically switches to later-refund (at the airport). Nothing to panic about at the register.
  • Items you already got the instant refund on have already had the tax removed. No need to scan those same items again at the airport.

③ The Airport Kiosk 6 Steps — Before You Check Your Baggage Is the Rule

"Check-in counter → refund kiosk → (customs counter) → pickup" is the entire sequence. No matter the airline or terminal, it never strays from this order.

Step Screen/Situation What to do Trap
1 Check-in counter Tell staff you have refund-eligible items before your checked baggage goes through ("tax refund items") Pack it in your suitcase and check it first, and the scan itself becomes impossible — order is everything
2 Refund kiosk, 「yeogwon seukaen (여권 스캔) — Passport scan」 Place your passport on the scanner Supports 4 languages (EN/KR/中/日) and runs 24 hours, so timing isn't a worry
3 「yeongsujeung/QR seukaen (영수증/QR 스캔) — Receipt/QR scan」 Scan the refund slip or QR you got from the store Multiple slips? Scan them one at a time, in order
4 「hwangeup sudan seontaek (환급 수단 선택) — Choose refund method」 Choose among Cash (현금) (KRW/USD/CNY) · Card (카드) · Digital Wallet (디지털 지갑) Cash is instant on the spot, card takes anywhere from instant to several days
5 Whether 「segwan hwagin daesang (세관 확인 대상) — Customs check required」 appears If it shows, head to the customs counter; if not, you're done This mostly applies only to high-value or inspection-flagged items — no display means it's already over
6 suryeong (수령) — Pickup Head to the terminal-specific route (see ④) T1/T2 routes differ, so check ahead of time

④ The Customs Branch — When the 「Customs Check Required」 Screen Appears

「segwan hwagin daesang (세관 확인 대상) — Customs check required」 mostly shows up for high-value items or ones flagged for inspection. When this screen appears, head to the nearby customs counter and get your items checked. The staffed counter runs 07:00–22:00, so it's safer to arrive within that window. If it doesn't appear, you don't need to go to customs at all — the procedure is simply done. No display at all is normal, not a sign something's wrong.

⑤ T1/T2 Pickup Routes — Different Terminal, Different Route

  • T1: Scan near counters J/L → cash pickup machine near Gate 28
  • T2: Scan near D/E → pickup near Gate 249

Check which terminal your departure airline uses ahead of time so you're not wandering near the gate. Terminal confirmation and check-in flow connect to #1293 Departure Self-Check-in Guide.

⑥ 12 Kiosk Screen Survival Words

Romanization (Korean) Meaning
yeogwon seukaen (여권 스캔) Passport scan — the step where you place your passport
yeongsujeung seukaen (영수증 스캔) Receipt scan — scanning the refund slip
kyu-a seukaen (QR 스캔) QR scan — for when it's a QR instead of a slip
segwan hwagin daesang (세관 확인 대상) Customs check required — head to the customs counter
hyeon-geum (현금) Cash — instant pickup
kadeu (카드) Card — instant to several days
dijiteol jigap (디지털 지갑) Digital wallet — refund to your app account
da-eum (다음) Next — moves to the next screen
wallyo (완료) Complete/Done — procedure finished
chwiso (취소) Cancel — start over from the beginning
witak-suhamul (위탁수하물) Checked baggage — the bags you check in
suryeong (수령) Collection/Pickup — where you get your refund

Tip: you don't need to memorize all 12. Knowing just four gets the whole procedure into view — segwan hwagin daesang (세관 확인 대상) (branch decision) · hyeon-geum (현금)/kadeu (카드) (choosing your method) · wallyo (완료) (the signal that you're done).

⑦ 3 Panic Scenarios

1. You already packed your refund items and checked the bag — if you haven't left the check-in counter yet, go back and say "tax refund items." They'll tag it and hand the bag back, so as long as it hasn't gone through, you can always undo it.

2. The 「Customs check required」 screen appeared and you don't know where to go — just head to the nearby customs counter. It only means a high-value or inspection-flagged item, not that something went wrong.

3. You find out you've exceeded the instant-refund limit right at the register — go over ₩1,000,000 per transaction or ₩5,000,000 cumulative, and it just auto-switches to later-refund (airport processing) — the refund itself isn't cancelled.
