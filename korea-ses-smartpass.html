🎯 Know This First — "SES" Is Actually Two Different Things

There is a reason travel posts and blogs list registration hours as 07–18, 07–22, or 24 hours. The automated clearance you use on arrival and the one you use on departure are different systems, with different registration locations and hours. On top of that, Smart Pass (face-authenticated departure), run by Incheon Airport, gets mixed in and makes it more confusing.

Type What it is Who Where Status
Arrival auto clearance Immigration on arrival through automated gates Citizens of 42 countries/regions, aged 17+, entering visa-free Incheon T1 and T2, Jeju arrivals hall Official (Ministry of Justice)
Departure auto clearance Immigration on departure through automated gates Short-term visitors aged 17+ who provided fingerprints and face on arrival Departure immigration area Official (Ministry of Justice)
Smart Pass Register your face, then pass the departure hall and boarding gates Aged 7+ (for foreigners, see ③ below) Incheon T1 and T2 departures Official (Incheon International Airport Corporation)

① Arrival Auto Clearance — Shorter Lines If You're From One of the 42

It started with 4 countries/regions (Germany, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macao) on 2025-11-03, grew to 18 on 2025-12-01, and reached 42 countries/regions on 2026-03-16 (Ministry of Justice). During the 2026 Chuseok holiday (Sept 24–27), 62.3% of the 777,766 travelers (Korean and foreign) passing through immigration at Incheon Airport used the automated gates (Ministry of Justice, 2026-10-01).

The 42 eligible countries/regions (Ministry of Justice data, 2026-06-01)

  • Asia, Middle East, Americas, Oceania: Japan, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macao, Singapore, UAE, Mexico, Canada, Australia, New Zealand
  • Europe: Germany, United Kingdom, France, Italy, Spain, Portugal, Netherlands, Belgium, Luxembourg, Ireland, Austria, Switzerland, Liechtenstein, Czech Republic, Hungary, Poland, Slovakia, Slovenia, Croatia, Finland, Sweden, Denmark, Norway, Iceland, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, Greece, Malta, Cyprus, Romania, Bulgaria

⚠ The United States and mainland China are not on the 42-country list — expect the regular immigration line on arrival (treatment of U.S. SES reciprocal enrollees is not officially confirmed). Departure auto clearance is still possible, as described in ②.

How to use it for the first time

1. When you reach the immigration hall, follow the "Automated Immigration Registration" signs to the registration line

2. Scan your passport → fingerprint → face photo → registration complete

3. Pass through the automated gate. They put an SES sticker in your passport; it is marked on your next visit, so don't peel it off (a real example from a Japanese travel blog)

  • Under 17 cannot use it. Families with a child have to send only the child through the regular line, so it is easier to wait in the regular line together.
  • We could not confirm the registration counter hours in official documents. Secondary sources list T1 07–22 and T2 24 hours/05–20, but on-site accounts conflict — if you land late at night, expect the regular line.
  • No registration fee is listed on the official page (secondary sources say it is free). In peak season the registration line itself can be long (March 2026 news report: a case of a 1–2 hour wait).

② Departure Auto Clearance — If You Were Fingerprinted and Photographed on Arrival, No Registration Needed

Short-term visitors aged 17+ who provided fingerprints and a face scan on arrival, by any method, can use the automated gates on departure without pre-registration (Ministry of Justice). Mainland China and U.S. passport holders also qualify if their fingerprints and face were captured at regular immigration on arrival.

  • Using the automated gate: green-arrow gate → place the passport photo page on the reader (2–3 seconds) → door opens → fingerprint → camera
  • If you still want to register on the departure side: Incheon T1 3rd floor, opposite Counter H, or T2 2nd floor, Ministry of Justice Immigration Service Center, both 07:00–18:00 every day, departing passengers only (Incheon International Airport Corporation)
  • Departure auto clearance operates at major airports and ports including Incheon, Gimpo, Gimhae, Jeju, Daegu and Cheongju (Ministry of Justice)

③ Smart Pass — Pass the Departure Hall and Boarding Gate with Your Face

Smart Pass is an Incheon International Airport Corporation service, separate from the Ministry of Justice's auto clearance. If you register your passport, face and boarding pass in advance, you pass through a dedicated departure-hall lane and some boarding gates by face authentication.

  • It is for departure only. It is not used for arrival. Incheon T1 and T2 only
  • You still go through Ministry of Justice departure immigration separately. "Leaving without a passport, using only your face" is wrong — bring your physical passport and boarding pass
  • Aged 7+ (legal guardian consent for under 14), face data valid for 5 years, re-register if you get a new passport
  • Registration: Smart Pass app, airport kiosks, partner bank/pay apps
  • Boarding-gate face authentication works only with participating airlines — T1: Jeju Air (제주항공), Air Premia (에어프레미아), Eastar Jet (이스타항공), Trinity (트리니티), Cathay Pacific (캐세이퍼시픽), EVA Air (에바항공) / T2: Korean Air (대한항공), Asiana (아시아나), Jin Air (진에어), Air Seoul (에어서울), Air Busan (에어부산). The dedicated departure-hall lane works regardless of airline

⚠ The official page does not say whether foreigners can use it. A 2023 news report citing the corporation's announcement said "aged 7+, Korean and foreign nationals," while a 2026-06 report said "Korean citizens," so they conflict. If the registration screen rejects a foreign passport, just go to the regular departure hall. Inquiries: Incheon Airport Customer Center 1577-2600.

⚠ 5 Traps That Actually Catch People

1. Treating arrival SES and departure SES as the same thing — the registration location, hours and eligibility differ.

2. Lining up for auto clearance with a child — under 17 cannot use arrival auto clearance.

3. Looking for the departure-side registration center at night — 07–18, departing passengers only.

4. Believing Smart Pass means no passport needed — departure immigration is separate; passport and boarding pass are required.

5. Standing in the auto clearance line when you're not from the 42 — with U.S. and mainland China passports, expect the regular line on arrival (departure auto clearance is possible).

⚙ FAQ 3

1. I have a Japanese passport and it's my first visit. Can I use auto clearance right away? — On your first arrival, register your fingerprints and face at the registration line, and from that day you can use the automated gates (aged 17+).

2. Is this related to K-ETA or e-Arrival? — They are separate systems. For entry paperwork, see the #1289 e-Arrival, #248 Visa and K-ETA guides.

3. I'm arriving at Gimpo or Gimhae. — Foreigner registration for arrival auto clearance operates at Incheon and Jeju arrivals halls (Ministry of Justice data, 2026-06). Departure auto clearance also works at Gimpo and Gimhae. Once registered at Incheon or Jeju, you can use the automated gates again when arriving at regional airports such as Gimpo and Gimhae (Ministry of Justice press release: 66.6% reuse rate at regional airports).
