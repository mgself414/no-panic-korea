About

No-Panic Korea is a set of survival manuals for first-time visitors to Korea. Not recommendations — operating manuals: what the screen shows, which button to press, in what order. Read one in bed before your flight, and the machine won't be a stranger.

Every manual follows the same notation: buttons as Takeout (포장), on-screen text as 「Select order type」, physical actions in bold.
